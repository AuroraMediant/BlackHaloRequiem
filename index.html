Black Halo Requiem

Hope Immortal

A mythic dark fantasy, mostly featuring fallen angels. Baroque gothic opulence steeped in Roman elements. Think Star Wars meets Paradise Lost.

“Do you think there is anything out there, beyond what’s here... anything beyond death?” Gabriel warms his hands near the campfire and glances at the blanket of stars resting above.

“Of course,” Dawn replies.

“You say that with such certainty.”

“There are some things you just know.”

The fate of worlds are thrown off balance when, deep in a secluded forest, Gabriel meets a mysterious woman. Powerful forces watch from afar as their love blooms, but all is not as it seems.

Journey into the dark and fantastical realms of forgotten angels, where power is everything and freedom is nothing. In these cold and unforgiving lands, there is but one path: the path of the sword.

Reader discretion is advised.

About Aurora

Aurora Mediant is a mythic dark fantasy author. Her father was a United Methodist minister, and her mother is from the Philippines.

Born in Kansas, Aurora grew up largely in Minnesota and currently resides in Hastings, Nebraska. She is a highly introverted nerd who gravitates towards science, comics, sci-fi, and history.

Aurora Mediant suffers from schizoaffective bipolar manic type disorder, social pragmatic communication disorder, and cPTSD. Auditory hallucinations, colloquially known as 'hearing voices', have been somewhat advantageous to her writing.

The name Mediant was chosen in honor of my best friend who died in 2018. Rest in peace my friend.
